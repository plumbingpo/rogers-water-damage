Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Rogers

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
