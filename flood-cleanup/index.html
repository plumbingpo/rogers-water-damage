Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. For flood emergencies anywhere in Rogers, Rogers Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property

How to Tell You Need Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
