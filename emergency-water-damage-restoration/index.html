Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.

Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

What a Professional Brings to Emergency Water Damage Restoration

In an emergency, the hard part is not mopping up what you can see. What takes skill is figuring out where everything else has soaked in.
