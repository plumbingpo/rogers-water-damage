Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Rogers Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Rogers using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Program (503) 565-2149 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor

Plumbing lines and electrical conduit carry it farther, and drywall wicks it upward. Finding it is a job for moisture detection tools in the hands of IICRC-certified technicians.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
