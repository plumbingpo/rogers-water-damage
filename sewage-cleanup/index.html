Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Rogers Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Rogers, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
