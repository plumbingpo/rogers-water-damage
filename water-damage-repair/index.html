Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • When kitchens, bathrooms or laundry rooms are rebuilt, choose backer board and moisture resistant drywall.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Rogers

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Wall cavities remain open where drywall was removed for drying.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Grout is breaking up and some tiles have lifted where moisture reached the floor below.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
